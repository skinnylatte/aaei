Our workshops now come with three practice labs, so participants can test real failure modes rather than read about them.

- **Northstar Air.** An airline support agent that looks up bookings and changes where itineraries are sent. Find where it fails, then prove the fix.
- **Harbour City.** A multilingual public services assistant. Ask the same question in five ways, including Chinese, Malay, Tamil and mixed language, and compare the answers.
- **Uncle Lim's secret.** A prompt injection game against a real AI model. Get a hawker to give away his secret ingredient.

The labs are for workshop participants, who [sign in](/case-studies/) with the password we share on the day.
