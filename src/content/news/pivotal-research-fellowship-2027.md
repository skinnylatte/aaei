[Pivotal Research](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/XWom297KDJXkirfiC/pivotal-research-fellowship-q1-2027-applications-open) is taking applications for its Q1 2027 fellowship: 15 weeks of full-time, mentored research in London, from 18 January to 30 April 2027.

- **Areas:** technical AI safety, technical governance and AI governance
- **Support:** a stipend of £15,000–18,000 depending on seniority, plus £1,800 a month for housing
- **Who can apply:** anyone from first-year undergraduates to experienced professionals, including people without a degree. Fit with a mentor matters more than credentials.
- **Deadline:** 1 November 2026, 23:59 AoE

[Apply here](https://apply.pivotal-research.org/).
