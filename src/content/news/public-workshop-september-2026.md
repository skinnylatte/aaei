On 29 September we ran a public edition of Break AI Before Deploying AI in Singapore: a hands-on introduction to evaluating and red teaming generative and agentic AI systems before deployment.

Participants worked in interdisciplinary groups, because effective evaluation needs technical, policy, legal and operational expertise. The workshop covers how to:

- identify consequential failure modes
- run structured adversarial tests
- test tools, permissions and oversight
- turn findings into repeatable evaluations
- evaluate mitigations and regressions
- build evidence for deployment decisions
