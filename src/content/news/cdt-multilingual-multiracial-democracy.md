How can AI support a multilingual and multiracial democracy when language can shape who benefits from new technology?

The [Center for Democracy & Technology](https://cdt.org/), GW Law's Multiracial Democracy Project and the Bernard Center for Law & Technology are hosting a half-day symposium on that question. It brings together experts on multilingual AI, language equity and civil rights, and on the barriers people face when they use AI systems in languages other than English.

- **When:** Monday 28 September 2026, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.
- **Where:** Moot Court Room, George Washington University Law School, 2000 H St NW, Washington, D.C.
- **Cost:** free and open to the public
