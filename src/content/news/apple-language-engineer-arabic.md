Apple's Global Siri team is hiring a [Language Engineer for Arabic](https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/details/200683776-0215/language-engineer-arabic-global-siri?team=MLAI) in Barcelona, posted on 15 September 2026.

- Be the technical lead for the Arabic market, across speech recognition, language understanding and responses.
- Work with large language models, agents and data generation, so Siri feels native to Arabic speakers.
- Needs professional-level Arabic, deep familiarity with the culture of Arabic-speaking regions, and strong machine learning or software engineering, with working knowledge of the other.

As AI assistants reach more of the world, the people who make them work in each language and culture are becoming some of the most important engineers in the field. Roles like this one are where that work happens.
