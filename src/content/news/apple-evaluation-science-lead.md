Apple is hiring an [Evaluation Science Lead](https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/details/200686562-0836/evaluation-science-lead?team=MLAI) in Cupertino, posted on 9 October 2026, to define how its multilingual Globalization AI is measured across Apple Music, the App Store, Apple TV+, Apple Podcasts and more.

- Own evaluation strategy for AI across 50+ languages and dozens of markets, including targeted approaches for low-resource locales.
- Design statistically sound evaluation: sampling, power analysis, confidence intervals and significance thresholds.
- Combine human annotation, with calibration and inter-annotator agreement, with automated scoring such as LLM-as-judge, and tell real regressions from measurement noise.
- Needs 5+ years in evaluation science, data science or ML systems, and hands-on experience with annotator agreement and measurement validity. Applied linguistics and generative AI evaluation are a plus.
- Onsite, with up to 10% travel. Base pay $175,500 to $311,700.

Multilingual evaluation is becoming a discipline of its own: not just checking translations, but measuring whether people in every language and market are served well. This role is built around exactly that.
