The [call for papers for ACL 2027](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/) is open. The conference runs from 20 to 22 August 2027, and papers are due through ACL Rolling Review by **4 January 2027**.

Two parts of the call fit multilingual evaluation work closely:

- **Language Diversity and NLP for Low-resourced Languages**, and **Multilingualism and Cross-Lingual NLP**, among the submission areas.
- **A special theme on homogenisation and knowledge collapse in LLMs**, which asks, among other things, whether LLMs produce more uniform text in lower-resource languages.

ACL 2027 workshops will publish their own calls from late November 2026.
